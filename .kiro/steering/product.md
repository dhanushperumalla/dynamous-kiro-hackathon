# Product Overview

## Product Purpose
AI-Sikshak is an AI-powered career mentorship platform that helps confused students and graduates find their career direction. When students are uncertain about their future, AI-Sikshak analyzes their interests, suggests relevant domains, creates personalized learning roadmaps with weekly targets, sends progress notifications, and connects them with job opportunities upon completion.

## Target Users
- **Recent Graduates**: Students who have completed their education but are unsure about career direction
- **Jobless Students**: Inndividuals actively seeking employmet but lacking clear career focus
- **Career-Confused Students**: Students at any stage who need guidance on career paths and skill development
- **Career Changers**: Professionals looking to transition into new domains

**User Needs:**
- Clear career direction and guidance
- Structured learning paths with achievable milestones
- Motivation and accountability through progress tracking
- Direct connection to relevant job opportunities
- Personalized recommendations based on individual interests

## Key Features
- **Interest Assessment**: Comprehensive questionnaire to identify student interests and aptitudes
- **Domain Recommendation Engine**: AI-powered suggestions for career domains based on interests
- **Personalized Learning Roadmaps**: Detailed plans to master chosen domains with structured progression
- **Weekly Target System**: Bite-sized, achievable goals with clear deadlines
- **Progress Notifications**: Automated reminders and motivational messages
- **Job Integration**: Direct links to relevant job postings upon course completion
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual dashboards showing learning journey and achievements
- **Responsive Web Access**: Available on desktop and mobile browsers for flexible learning

## Business Objectives
- **Student Success**: Help 10,000+ students find clear career direction within first year
- **Completion Rates**: Achieve 70%+ course completion rate through effective engagement
- **Job Placement**: Connect 60%+ of completers with relevant job opportunities
- **User Engagement**: Maintain high daily active usage through effective notification system
- **Platform Growth**: Scale to support multiple domains and career paths

## User Journey
1. **Onboarding**: Student signs up and completes comprehensive interest assessment
2. **Domain Discovery**: AI analyzes responses and presents 3-5 relevant career domains
3. **Path Selection**: Student chooses preferred domain and reviews detailed learning roadmap
4. **Learning Phase**: Student follows weekly targets with regular progress notifications
5. **Skill Building**: Continuous learning with milestone celebrations and progress tracking
6. **Job Readiness**: Upon completion, student receives curated job listings and application support
7. **Career Launch**: Student applies to jobs with confidence and structured preparation

## Success Criteria
- **User Satisfaction**: 4.5+ star rating and positive feedback on career guidance quality
- **Learning Effectiveness**: Students report increased confidence and clarity about career direction
- **Completion Metrics**: High course completion rates and consistent weekly target achievement
- **Job Success**: Measurable job placement rates and career advancement for users
- **Platform Engagement**: Strong daily/weekly active user metrics and low churn rates
